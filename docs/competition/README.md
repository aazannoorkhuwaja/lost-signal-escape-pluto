## Schedule and live demonstration

Use the shorter published schedule unless the organizers clarify it. The mobile guidelines reserve the first 15 minutes for briefing, the next 90 for development and the next 15 for submission. Upload closes **two hours after the official start**. The starter guide instead lists 8:30 AM–12:30 PM with three hours of coding. The team confirmed the cutoff on site.

Allow three minutes for the live demo and one minute for Q&A. Use the submitted build, explain the theme and show the core gameplay loop. The mobile guidelines prohibit changes after the submission cutoff. Bring student IDs, the configured laptop, charger, Android phone, USB cable and headphones.

## Judging

The mobile guidelines award 25 points for gameplay and fun, 20 for theme, 20 for creativity, 15 for mobile design and controls, 10 for technical execution and polish, and 10 for presentation. Ties go first to gameplay, then theme. The starter guide instead awards 25 each for creativity and gameplay, 20 for theme and 15 each for visual presentation and completeness.
